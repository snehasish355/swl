How this recommendation works

A band recommendation is an informed starting point, not a reception guarantee. Actual results depend on the path, transmitter schedule and direction, antenna, interference, and live ionospheric conditions.

This page uses a simple solar geometry and day/night rule of thumb: higher HF bands tend to favor daylight paths, lower bands tend to favor darkness, with roughly 9–12 MHz often bridging both. Solar flux nudges the suggested range; elevated K-index and storm noise add caution. It does not fetch live space-weather measurements or predict individual paths.
